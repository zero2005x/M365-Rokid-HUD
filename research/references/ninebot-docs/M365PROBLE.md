## Xiaomi M365 Pro BLE (Head board)

### MCU I/O ports
 I/O  | Pin | Mode | Periph | Function 
----- | --- | ---- | ------ | --------
 AIN1 |  46 | AIN  | ADC    | Throttle position sensor
 AIN0 |  45 | AIN  | ADC    | Brake position sensor
 AIN2 |  5  | AIN  | ADC    | ACH0
 AIN3 |  6  | AIN  | ADC    | ACH3
 P0.08 | 14 | I | GPIO | Board type (0-Pro/1-Classic)
 P0.25 | 44 | O | GPIO | Beeper
 P0.03 | 7 | O | GPIO | Headlight
 P0.04 | 8 | IO | GPIO | Display I2C SDA
 P0.05 | 9 | O |GPIO | Display I2C SCL
 P0.15 | 21 | IO | UART | Communication bus
